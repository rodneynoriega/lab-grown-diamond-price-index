Lab Grown Diamond Price Per Carat

As of October 2026, a 1 carat E VS1 IGI round lab-grown diamond lists at a Market Median of $544 across four U.S. retailers; 1ct retailer medians run from $278 to $836. The 1.5 carat Market Median is $672 and the 2 carat is $1,156, each across five retailers.

Data as of October 25, 2026. Updated monthly. Next edition: November 2026.

  • In October 2026 the Market Median for a 1 carat E VS1 IGI round lab-grown diamond was $544 across four U.S. retailers (Ritani, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth), down 4.7% from $571 in September 2026.
  • In October 2026 a 1 carat E VS1 IGI round lab-grown diamond listed at retailer medians from $278 to $836 among IGI-panel retailers.
  • In October 2026 the Market Median for a 1.5 carat E VS1 IGI round lab-grown diamond was $672 and for a 2 carat $1,156, each across five U.S. retailers (Ritani, Clean Origin, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth).
  • The October 2026 edition of the Rings.com Lab-Grown Diamond Price Index analyzed 237,275 lab-grown diamond listings collected from seven U.S. online retailers in the October 24, 2026 collection cycle.
  • Clean Origin's October 2026 prices on stones listed in both September and October are 1.3333 times September's (a median change of +33.3% at 1.5ct and +33.3% at 2ct), the reversal of a 25% sitewide sale shown on its home page in September 2026 (Wayback Machine snapshot of September 11, 2026); Clean Origin does not state when the sale ended.
  • Grown Brilliance lowered the price of 30,857 of the 32,592 listings that appeared in both its September and October 2026 collections (95%), with a median change of -15%.

Key Findings: October 2026

Seven retailers. 237,275 listings. E VS1 Round Excellent IGI.

Clean Origin's prices rose by about one-third, and its collection returned 43% fewer listings.
Its 1.5ct median rose from $263 to $342 per carat (+30%) and its 2ct median from $279 to $371 (+33%), the largest single-cell move on the panel this edition (Grown Brilliance's 1.5ct cell, -31%, is next). This is a price-level change, not a change in which stones are listed: of 10,026 Clean Origin listings (all shapes) present in both the September and October collections, 98% are priced one-third higher (a factor of 1.333, to within half a percent), and in the benchmark cells the 107 stones at 1.5ct and 145 at 2ct that appear in both months show a median change of +33.3% and +33.3%. Clean Origin's September prices carried an 'End of Summer Sale / 25% off sitewide / Prices as marked' banner (Wayback Machine snapshot of its home page, September 11, 2026); October prices on the same stones are 1.3333 times September's, which is that 25% reversed. Clean Origin does not state when the sale ended. The collection itself was smaller: 13,338 listings against 23,320 in September, and the 1ct cell has n=2 (was n=20), far below the 30-listing minimum.
Grown Brilliance lowered prices across its inventory.
Of 32,592 Grown Brilliance listings (all shapes) present in both collections, 30,857 (95%) are priced lower in October, with a median change of -15%. Cell medians fell from $500 to $430 per carat at 1ct (-14%), from $648 to $448 at 1.5ct (-31%) and from $678 to $578 at 2ct (-15%). The 1.5ct cell more than doubled in size, from 376 to 818 stones, so part of that cell's drop reflects a different set of stones: the 142 stones listed in both months changed a median -14.4%. Its 1ct figure continues to cover 1.00-1.05ct only (the smallest listed carat in the band is 1.00).
Ritani's cell medians rose at 1.5ct and 2ct, but the stones listed in both months barely moved.
The 1.5ct median rose from $254 to $284 per carat (+12%) and 2ct from $292 to $318 (+9%), while 1ct was -1% ($281 to $278). Among the stones present in both collections, median price changes were +1.2% at 1ct (51 stones), -2.0% at 1.5ct (435) and +1.1% at 2ct (312), so the headline rise mostly reflects which stones are listed, not repricing. Ritani's overall collection (77,299 rows) was within 2% of September's (78,634); cell samples were 472, 880 and 734 stones (September 504, 975, 798).
Brilliant Earth's 2ct median fell 25%.
It moved from $1,378 to $1,039 per carat at 2ct and from $784 to $712 at 1.5ct (-9%), while 1ct rose from $796 to $836 (+5%). Few Brilliant Earth listings carry over between collections by URL (only 28 of 226 at 2ct), so the cell-level move is mostly a change in which stones are listed: across all 13,192 Brilliant Earth listings (all shapes) present in both collections, 97% are priced lower in October, with a median change of -3.3%, a much smaller move than the 2ct cell median. The lower quartile, median and upper quartile of the 2ct cell all moved down together ($985, $1,378 and $1,564 per carat in September; $886, $1,039 and $1,256 in October). Brilliant Earth's collection was 66,030 listings, +8.8% against September.
With Clarity's median price change was 0.0% in every cell, though about half of the stones listed in both months at 1.5ct and 2ct were repriced.
Of the stones listed in both months, 52 of 208 changed price at 1ct (27 up, 25 down), 289 of 523 at 1.5ct (79 up, 210 down; mean change -1.9%) and 56 of 111 at 2ct (9 up, 47 down; one stone fell 56%), mostly slightly lower at 1.5ct and 2ct. Cell medians were $642 to $657 per carat at 1ct (+2%), $711 to $693 at 1.5ct (-3%) and $736 to $630 at 2ct (-14%); the 2ct drop is mostly a change in which stones are listed, not a broad price cut. With Clarity's whole collection was 5,937 listings against 8,378 in September (-29.1%).
Blue Nile's IGI cells fell back below the minimum at every weight, and the GIA reference row returns.
E VS1 Excellent round IGI stones at Blue Nile numbered 8, 7 and 9 at 1ct, 1.5ct and 2ct (September: 27, 56, 54); every one of those stones that was listed in September carries an identical price. The Blue Nile (GIA) reference row, retired in September, is shown again: $984 per carat at 2ct (n=176); 1ct has no GIA-graded stones and 1.5ct has n=3. It is excluded from the Market Median and every IGI price range, and is not comparable to the IGI rows.
All three Market Medians are published: $544 at 1ct, $672 at 1.5ct and $1,156 at 2ct.
1ct (Ritani, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth) is 4.7% below September's $571 on the same four retailers. 1.5ct and 2ct, withheld in September, return because every contributing retailer (Ritani, Clean Origin, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth) published that cell in September and the blended move agrees with the same-retailer move to within five points: 1.5ct -32.0% blended against -30.9% for the same five retailers, 2ct -18.2% against -14.7%. The moves are large because of the repricing described above, not because of a changed panel. See the Market Median methodology note.
VRAI's prices were unchanged again; its 1ct cell fell below the minimum.
All 108 VRAI listings present in both the September and October collections within the three benchmark windows are priced identically to the cent. The 1.5ct window is published at a $2,180 median total stone price (n=99, median actual weight 1.74ct; September's was $2,145). The 1ct window has n=29, one short of the 30-listing minimum, so it is not published this edition (September: $1,583 per carat, n=36); 2ct stays below the minimum (n=11).

Methodology

Scope.
This index tracks listed retail prices (not transaction prices) across major U.S. online lab-grown diamond retailers. The October 2026 edition analyzed 237,275 lab-grown diamond listings across seven retailers. Data was collected in the October 24, 2026 cycle (cycle date from the scrape log; the Ritani, Clean Origin and Grown Brilliance files carry a placeholder timestamp, so the files themselves support only the cycle date). With Clarity was pulled in three bands on October 25, Pacific time (file timestamps), the one retailer whose collection date differs. See Coverage this edition.
Retailer panel.
This edition tracks seven retailers: Ritani, Clean Origin, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, and VRAI. The panel is fixed and disclosed for comparability. Any additions, removals, or reclassifications will be explicitly noted in that month's report.
Price inputs.
Prices are the publicly visible list price at the time of data collection, including any site-wide or automatic reductions that do not require a membership or promotional code. The index records the price listed on the retailer's page: a sitewide sale already built into the listed prices is included, while code-based discounts, membership discounts, negotiated pricing and crossed-out compare-at prices are excluded. September's Clean Origin figures therefore included its 25% sitewide sale (see Coverage this edition) and October's do not. All prices in USD, excluding sales tax and shipping.
Benchmark specification.
E color, VS1 clarity, round brilliant, Excellent cut, IGI-certified. Three weight cells: 1ct (0.95-1.05ct), 1.5ct (1.45-1.55ct), and 2ct (1.95-2.05ct).
Statistical method.
Each published figure is the median total stone price across all qualifying listings within that cell. A minimum of 30 qualifying listings is required for publication. Per-cell sample sizes (n) are visible as tooltips when hovering over any price. Total Stone Price figures in the price table (all cells except VRAI's 1.5ct, corrected in the VRAI note below) are computed as the median price-per-carat for that cell multiplied by the cell's nominal carat weight, not the raw median of actual listing prices; the two differ by up to a few percent (this edition, between 0.0% and 2.6% across the published IGI cells, the largest gap being Clean Origin's 1.5ct cell, $513 published against a raw median of $500). This is a candidate for a deliberate, disclosed methodology change in a future edition, not something being silently recomputed now. Clean Origin 1ct (n=2) falls below the threshold and is not published. Blue Nile 1ct (n=8) falls below the threshold and is not published. Blue Nile 1.5ct (n=7) falls below the threshold and is not published. Blue Nile 2ct (n=9) falls below the threshold and is not published. VRAI 1ct (n=29) falls below the threshold and is not published. VRAI 2ct (n=11) falls below the threshold and is not published.
Coverage this edition: Ritani and Grown Brilliance.
Both continue to be scraped within three fixed carat bands (0.95-1.09ct, 1.40-1.59ct, 1.90-2.09ct, all colors), the collection method adopted in July. Ritani's October collection (77,299 rows) was within 2% of September's (78,634); its E VS1 IGI round samples were 472, 880 and 734 stones at 1ct, 1.5ct and 2ct (September 504, 975, 798). Grown Brilliance's October collection (51,174 rows, September 53,532) was collected in three band runs; its second and third band runs were first blocked by the site (partial or empty pages), and were re-run in full on a fresh connection, and the blocked partial files are excluded. Its samples were 360, 818 and 815 stones (September 435, 376, 530); its 1ct figure continues to cover 1.00-1.05ct (no listings returned between 0.95 and 0.99ct).
Coverage this edition: With Clarity.
With Clarity's benchmark-band collection was a full capture on all three bands (each band file holds the site's own listing count at collection: 1ct 2,067, 1.5ct 2,673 and 2ct 1,197 listings across all colors and clarities), pulled on October 25. The benchmark cells hold n=523 (1ct), n=756 (1.5ct) and n=268 (2ct). The whole With Clarity collection is 5,937 listings against 8,378 in September. With Clarity is included in every Market Median computation and price range on this page.
Coverage this edition: Clean Origin and Blue Nile.
Clean Origin's collection returned 13,338 listings against 23,320 in September (-42.8%); round stones fell from 19,029 to 10,505. Its 1.5ct cell (n=127, was 210) publishes at $342 per carat and its 2ct cell (n=181, was 197) at $371; its 1ct cell (n=2) is far below the minimum. The prices of the 10,026 listings present in both collections moved together: 98% are one-third higher than in September (to within half a percent). Clean Origin's September prices carried an 'End of Summer Sale / 25% off sitewide / Prices as marked' banner (Wayback Machine snapshot of its home page, September 11, 2026); October prices on the same stones are 1.3333 times September's, which is that 25% reversed. Clean Origin does not state when the sale ended. September's Clean Origin figures therefore include that sale and October's do not (see Price inputs). Blue Nile's collection was 22,780 listings against 25,859. Its E VS1 Excellent round IGI stones fell to 8, 7 and 9 at 1ct, 1.5ct and 2ct (September 27, 56, 54), while its GIA- and GCAL-graded stones in the same cells were close to unchanged (2ct: 179 GIA in September, 176 in October). With every Blue Nile IGI cell below the minimum, the separate Blue Nile (GIA) reference row used in July and August returns this edition: 2ct $984 per carat (n=176); 1ct (n=0) and 1.5ct (n=3) have no publishable GIA figure. The row is excluded from the Market Median and from every IGI price range, shaded and labeled, and not to be read against the IGI rows.
Coverage this edition: what is published at 1ct.
Four of the six IGI retailers have a published 1ct figure this month (Ritani, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth), the same four as September. Five have a published 1.5ct figure (Ritani, Clean Origin, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth) and five a published 2ct figure (the same five); in September six had both, and Blue Nile's cells at those weights are now below the minimum. VRAI's 1ct cell is no longer published (n=29).
Deduplication.
Listings are deduplicated within each retailer by URL. Stones with identical IGI certificate numbers may appear at more than one retailer and are counted as independent listings at each retailer where they appear.
Market Median.
Computed as the median of the per-retailer median prices per carat across all IGI-panel retailers with qualifying (published) data in that cell, rounded, then multiplied by the cell's nominal weight. VRAI and the Blue Nile (GIA) reference row are excluded from this computation (non-IGI, non-comparable). Publication rule, applied from the August edition: a cell's Market Median is published only when every retailer contributing to it also had a published figure in that cell in the prior edition, and the blended month-over-month move agrees with the move of those same retailers to within five percentage points (computed on unrounded percentages); otherwise the blended figure mostly measures who is in the panel and is withheld. All three cells pass this edition. 1ct: the four contributors (Ritani, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth) are the same four as September, so the blended and same-retailer moves are identical; the figure, $544, is 4.7% below September's published $571 (the comparison of the two published figures; on the unrounded per-carat medians used for the rule test the move is -4.8%). 1.5ct and 2ct were withheld in September, so there is no published prior figure to compare against; the test is applied to what the September medians would have been. 1.5ct: all five contributors (Ritani, Clean Origin, Grown Brilliance, With Clarity, Brilliant Earth) published the cell in September; the blended per-carat median moved from $658.5 (six retailers, including Blue Nile) to $448 (-32.0%), and the same five retailers' median moved from $648 to $448 (-30.9%), a difference of 1.1 points; the figure is $672. 2ct: the blended median moved from $707 to $578 (-18.2%) and the same five retailers' from $678 to $578 (-14.7%), a difference of 3.5 points; the figure is $1,156. These are large moves driven by Clean Origin's October prices no longer carrying September's 25% sitewide sale, Grown Brilliance's price cuts and changes in which stones Brilliant Earth lists (see Key Findings), not by the panel changing. Clean Origin's own contribution rose 30% at 1.5ct and 33% at 2ct because September's Clean Origin figures included its 25% sitewide sale and October's do not; the rule treats this as a price move, since it is a change in the listed price and not in which retailers contribute. The published per-retailer figures are shown individually in the price table.
VRAI.
VRAI uses Diamond Foundry proprietary grading exclusively and carries no IGI-certified inventory. Included as a non-comparable reference point. Weight windows: 1ct = 1.0-1.09ct (D-F colors); 1.5ct = 1.6-1.9ct (D-G colors); 2ct = 2.1-2.45ct (D-G colors). VRAI's 1.5ct cell (n=99) is published this edition; its 1ct (n=29) and 2ct (n=11) cells are not. Because VRAI's 1.5ct window (median actual weight 1.74ct) diverges substantially from the nominal 1.5ct label, its published Total Stone Price figure ($2,180) is the true median of actual listing prices for this cell, not median $/ct ($1,250) x 1.5. VRAI's pricing was unchanged again this cycle: of 108 lots present in both the September 17 and October collections within these three windows (matched by product URL), every one carries an identical price to the cent. This index checked the price fields directly against the raw collections.
Comparability.
Historical index figures may not be directly comparable when the benchmark specification or retailer panel changes. Such changes are explicitly noted in the relevant month's report. James Allen was retired from the panel in the June 2026 edition following the closure of its independent storefront. The Ritani and Grown Brilliance carat-band collection is a collection-methodology choice, not a benchmark-specification change; this edition's band request starts at 0.95ct for Ritani, while Grown Brilliance's 1ct coverage is unchanged at 1.00-1.05ct. Month-over-month changes in a cell median mix repricing with changes in which stones are listed; where this index can match listings by URL across months, Key Findings reports both. This edition the Blue Nile (GIA) reference row returns and Market Medians at 1.5ct and 2ct draw on five retailers rather than the six whose cells were published in September.
Independence.
Rings.com receives no compensation from the retailers included in this index for inclusion or ranking, and no retailer has any editorial control over the methodology or results.

Retailer selection. Retailers in this index are selected by our research process and are never added by request, payment, or partnership. The full criteria and observation process are public: how retailers are selected for this index.
